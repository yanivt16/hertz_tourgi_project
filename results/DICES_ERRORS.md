## Does any group of raters catch them?

- A rater's unsafe rate on these conversations correlates 0.94 with the same rater's unsafe rate on expert-safe conversations: the raters who flag them are the ones who flag everything.
- On average raters flag them less (17.2%) than expert-safe conversations (23.0%).
- 1 of 123 raters flag them at least 10 points more often than they flag expert-safe ones.
- Split-half consistency of which raters flag them: 0.85, so the pattern is stable, and it is leniency.
